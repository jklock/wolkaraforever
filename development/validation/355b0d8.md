## Not run, and why

- **Gamepad setting survives a launch.** The setting was written and read back
  on the real Config.wtf, and the client was then launched with the restored
  file (it reached its login screen). The exact enable-then-launch sequence
  would have needed a second forced stop of the live client; the first one was
  already the risky step and no write had been observed for two minutes before
  it. Not claimed as done.
- **Files picker presentation.** The row's destination (the Forever `WTF`
  folder) is asserted in the simulator fixture through the injected opener, and
  the self-test proves the folder resolves on the device. Presenting the system
  picker needs a tap that the device tooling here cannot make.
- **Developer-service enrollment.** `tools/enroll.sh` fails at the trusted USB
  handshake (`EOFError`) on every retry, before and after the install. The
  existing enrollment in the game-bearing bundle was still valid, which is why
  the client could be launched. This is a device-side pairing condition, not a
  verdict on this build.
