## Icons

The icons on TouchPlay's non-spell buttons (Bags, Menu and its tiles, Interact, Jump, Dive, Mount,
Next target, the minimap's mail ring) are from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) by Lorc, Delapouite
and Skoll, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
TouchPlay recoloured them as gold or red metal (a gradient, a bevel, an outline and a shadow) in
`Art/Glyphs.tga`, and turned the Jump arrow to point up. `Art/CREDITS.txt` lists the same.
